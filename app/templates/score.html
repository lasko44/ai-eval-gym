The Brier score is the mean of (probability - outcome) squared over the claims that resolved: 0.000 is perfect calibration and lower is better. 1.000 is the worst possible, and it is earned in either direction: a probability of 1.0 on a claim that resolved wrong, or a probability of 0.0 on a claim that resolved correct. The second shape is a confident denial of a problem that turned out to be real. Coverage is resolved claims over all claims on this cycle. A claim whose metric no harness run measured is unresolved: it is counted here, and left out of the Brier score entirely.
