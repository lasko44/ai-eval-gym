Add a claim

A claim is a prediction plus the number that would prove it wrong. Write the prediction in your own words, then name the measurement and the threshold that settles it. The form refuses a claim with no metric or no threshold. A prediction you cannot lose teaches you nothing.

I predict will come in
where 1 is certain it will happen and 0 is certain it will not

Metric names

Any name your harness reports will work. The library in this project can already compute these, so they cost you nothing to measure. The number after the name is yours to pick: a harness that reports recall at 5 calls it recall_at_5.

  • recall_at_k how much of what should have been found was found
  • precision_at_k how much of what came back belonged
  • ndcg_at_k whether the good results came back near the top
  • flip_rate how often a judge changes its mind when you swap the order

Seal

Sealing hashes these claims into a chain and commits the chain head to git. After that, nobody can add or edit a claim, and only then can the harness run. This is the step that makes the score mean something, and it cannot be undone.
