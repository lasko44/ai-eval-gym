One cycle is one teardown of someone else's AI system. You predict where it breaks before you measure it, then find out whether you were right. The order is enforced, not suggested: nothing can be measured until the predictions are sealed into git, and nothing can be scored until it has been measured.

  1. predict read the system and write what will fail, each with a metric and a threshold that would prove you wrong
  2. seal commit the claims to git, after which they cannot be edited
  3. measure write a harness, run it, and post its numbers
  4. score get a Brier score on your calibration, and a teardown you can publish
