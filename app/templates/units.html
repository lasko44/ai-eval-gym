Still to build

Five more units belong in parts 4 and 6, and nobody has written them yet. They cover model documentation scored against a fixed schema, and build against buy decided on stated criteria. They also cover fine tuning against prompting against retrieval on one task, third-party model validation, and the override criteria for an agent. Each one needs primary sources before it ships. So this page names them here instead of listing them above as though they existed.

One more is worth its own shape: the widely repeated claim that 95 percent of AI pilots fail. It comes from a 2025 MIT Media Lab Project NANDA report, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business. Secondary write-ups disagree about its sample, and MIT later restricted access to the report. I could not read the primary document, so this page quotes no figure from its methodology. To take that apart uses the same muscle as a retrieval pipeline. But the exercise is a document critique rather than a measurement, so it needs a different kind of check.
