They also cover query rewriting that degrades a well-formed query, semantic cache poisoning, and indirect prompt injection through retrieved content. The last two cover citations that resolve but do not support the claim, and observability that exports the content it should only trace.

One more unit is worth its own shape: the widely repeated claim that 95 percent of AI pilots fail. It comes from a 2025 MIT Media Lab Project NANDA report, The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business. Secondary write-ups disagree about its sample, and MIT later restricted access to the report. I could not read the primary document, so this page quotes no figure from its methodology. To take that apart uses the same muscle as a retrieval pipeline. But the exercise is a document critique and not a measurement. It needs a different kind of check from the units above.
