Units

One production failure mode per unit, paired with the measurement that catches it. You read what goes wrong, commit a prediction about it with a confidence, then implement the detection yourself against a fixture with a known answer.

Your calibration here is kept separate from your teardown score on purpose. Probes are cheap and numerous, teardown claims are few and expensive, so pooling them would give you a headline number that is mostly a quiz result.
